Privacy Policy

Application: Compass (package com.kont.compass)
Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Compass application handles. It is written to match the actual behaviour of the app, not a generic template.

1. No location access

Unlike many compass apps, this one does not request location permission and cannot see where you are. It reads only the magnetic sensor built into your device and shows the direction of magnetic north.

Because location is not used, true north (which requires knowing your position to calculate magnetic declination) is not offered.

2. Data the developer collects

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers and no analytics belonging to the developer. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores no personal data. Sensor readings are used to draw the dial and are immediately discarded; they are never written to storage or transmitted.

It keeps only two small records in its own private storage:

  • whether the short vibration when you reach the target direction is on or off,
  • usage counters that decide when to show the Google Play rating prompt (the number of separate days the app was opened, total time of use, and whether the prompt has already been shown).

These two records are included in Android's own backup and device-to-device transfer: if Google backup is turned on for your device, Android may back them up to your Google account. The developer cannot see them.

The vibration uses the system's haptic feedback and needs no additional permission.

4. Permissions

The app itself requests no permissions at all. Motion and magnetic sensors do not require a runtime permission on Android.

The following permissions are declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and appear in the app's manifest: internet access, network state, advertising ID and Android Ad Services permissions, and wake lock. The compass itself does not use the internet.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner advertisement supplied by Google AdMob. To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device information (model, operating system, language, screen size),
  • ad interaction data (whether an ad was shown, viewed or clicked).

This processing is carried out by Google under its own terms. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in a region where consent is required, the app shows a Google-certified consent screen (Google User Messaging Platform) the first time it runs. You can accept or refuse personalised advertising there. You can change your choice later with Ad privacy choices in the How it works window; this option appears only in regions where consent is required. If consent is not given, the compass still works in full.

Controlling advertising on your device

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (menu names vary by manufacturer).

6. Accuracy

Phone magnetometers are affected by metal, magnets, speakers, vehicle bodies and other electronics, and they need occasional recalibration. Readings are an estimate for everyday orientation and must not be relied upon for navigation, aviation, marine use or any situation where an error could cause harm.

7. Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect any data from children.

8. Data deletion

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request deletion of. Uninstalling the app removes everything it placed on your device.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: konttechnology@gmail.com

Hiçbiri. Uygulamada hesap, giriş, sunucu veya geliştiriciye ait analitik bulunmaz. Uygulamada yaptığınız hiçbir şey geliştiriciye gönderilmez.

3. Cihazınızda saklanan veriler

Uygulama kişisel veri saklamaz. Sensör okumaları yalnızca kadranı çizmek için kullanılır ve hemen atılır; hiçbir zaman kaydedilmez veya gönderilmez.

Kendi özel alanında yalnızca iki küçük kayıt tutar:

  • hedef yöne gelindiğinde verilen kısa titreşimin açık mı kapalı mı olduğu,

Uygulamanın kendisi hiçbir izin istemez. Hareket ve manyetik sensörler Android'de çalışma anı izni gerektirmez.

Manifestte görünen internet erişimi, ağ durumu, reklam kimliği, Android Ad Services ve wake lock izinleri Google Mobile Ads SDK'sı tarafından tanımlanır. Pusulanın kendisi internet kullanmaz.

Uygulama, Google AdMob tarafından sağlanan bir banner reklam gösterir. Reklam sunmak için Google Mobile Ads SDK'sı şunları işleyebilir:

  • IP adresinden türetilen yaklaşık konum,
  • cihaz bilgileri (model, işletim sistemi, dil, ekran boyutu),
  • reklam etkileşim verileri (gösterim, görüntüleme, tıklama).

Bu işleme Google tarafından kendi koşulları kapsamında yapılır: Google'ın uygulamalardan gelen bilgileri nasıl kullandığı ve Google Gizlilik Politikası.

Sonuçlar günlük yön bulma için bir tahmindir; denizcilik, havacılık, arazi navigasyonu gibi hatanın zarara yol açabileceği durumlarda güvenilmemelidir.
